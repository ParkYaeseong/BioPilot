# 17. Submission and demo readiness

## Minimum judging package

Public source repository with setup/dependency locks, an accessible three-minute video, an actual
working project and all contributors credited, following the final submission form [S21].
Do not call this design ZIP the completed product. Do not assert that a public website is mandatory
or that local execution is officially pre-approved without reading the final form.

Prepare a reproduction route: a small public sample, exact launch/setup instructions, allowed model
credentials through environment variables, known costs, and a read-only record viewer. Judges must
not receive the author's keys. A replay is an inspectable backup, not the only evidence of a runnable project.

## Readiness evidence

- Current rules and eligibility decision; exact new-work/reuse scope; permission and license ledger.
- Actual Meta provider/model/version and one scientific MCP trace, without hidden non-Meta fallback.
- Actual ESM artifacts; reference mapping checks; data-source/version notices and claim limitations.
- Fixed-vs-agent workflow evidence with scope and failures, or honest absence of this measurement.
- Real exported video metadata/audio/link check, not only a scripted 180-second outline.
- Repo/video/product links checked from an unauthenticated session or documented judge access.

## Suggested video plan

0-20: specific research bottleneck and output. 20-45: request/constraints.
45-80: real Meta-to-MCP scientific call. 80-110: meaningful constraint/recovery branch.
110-145: shortlist, evidence, actual/unknown phenotype and cost. 145-165: measured comparison.
165-180: new contribution, scope/limitations and reproducible handoff.

Time budgets are a recording plan. Never fill a results slide with invented numbers or a simulated
API response. Label time compression and cached replay. English captions are recommended for
clarity, not asserted as a rule. Review official language and duration requirements immediately before export.

## New-work provenance

Use compliance/originality_ledger.csv. Identify pre-event design/guard helpers, external models/data,
new event code, prompts and demos. Do not make previous work look new by changing file timestamps
or squashing away relevant history. Resolve ambiguous reuse with the organizers; document the answer.

## Permissions and submission

Prepare locally until explicit publication/deployment/submission authorization exists. Repository
visibility alone does not grant a license to institution-owned code/data. Remove credentials and
private account information. Record a real platform receipt only after a real user-authorized submission.
Leave submission/manifest.template.json status as not_submitted until then.
