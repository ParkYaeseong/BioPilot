## Track and theme

**Track: Indie Developer. Theme: Push the Frontier.** The two themes are not the two prize tracks.
Build for Your World emphasizes a real problem in one's domain. Push the Frontier includes using
Meta models for difficult software/physical/scientific problems and scientific workflow acceleration.
Our evidence should show Meta performing meaningful scientific tool coordination, not a claim of
inventing ESM or automatically discovering a higher-performing enzyme.

Both themes can fit a protein-review tool. Do not infer easier judging, theme quotas or guaranteed
acceptance. If the result is just convenient workflow packaging, acknowledge that and consider the
Build for Your World framing instead of overstating frontier science.

## Captured judging process

Stage 1 is a pass/fail theme and meaningful Meta-model-use screen. Stage 2 scores categories from
0 to 10. Stage 3 brings five projects per track to a final demo-based judging round.

| Category | Weight | BioPilot proof to prepare |
|---|---:|---|
| Impact | 30% | A concrete research job, measured time/steps or error handling on defined tasks |
| Meta AI Tooling | 25% | Actual model/tool trace; useful decisions, not a narration-only wrapper |
| Technical Depth & Execution | 20% | Correct scientific contracts, reliable MCP, state, budget and provenance |
| Demo | 25% | A comprehensible real working flow in three minutes |

These weights replace the earlier equal-25% assumption. Do not convert a self-review score into a
win probability. The application screening's 50/50 split is a different process.

## Submission requirements captured

Public repository; three-minute demo video with accessible link; working project; everyone credited;
one chosen track and one representative submission. Solo allowed, team size up to four in the guide.
Show only the features/code/functionality newly developed during the event as original event work.
The guide explicitly permits startups to build on existing products if new contributions are clear;
this is not a blanket permission to reuse an Indie entrant's pre-event implementation.

Do not treat these preparatory ZIPs or offline helpers as newly written competition code. Preserve
source dates and mark reuse in compliance/originality_ledger.csv. Do not backdate commits or delete
history. Public dependencies and data must have usable licenses and be identified.

A website/installer is not explicitly mandated by this snapshot. A local MCP product is our
interpretation, not an organizer ruling. Confirm any mandatory live-product URL, accessibility,
installation, video duration/language and reviewer-credential fields in the final form.

## Forbidden and risky positioning

The guide excludes policy/right violations and warns against generic medical advice, basic RAG,
simple image/education/health/sports or political/war applications. BioPilot is a benign enzyme
research tool, not patient diagnosis, therapy or medical advice. New integration alone is not proof
of originality or impact. Demonstrate the concrete computation and verification workflow.
